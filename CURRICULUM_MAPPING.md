# CURRICULUM MAPPING: NEP 2020 & NCF-SE 2023 ALIGNMENT
## MathQuest Classroom / CoveQuiz Platform

This document details the curricular alignment of all 160 interactive mathematical games built within COVEQUIZ. It reflects the core recommendations of India's **National Education Policy (NEP 2020)** and **National Curriculum Framework for School Education (NCF-SE 2023)**.

---

### Core Pedagogical Stages:
1. **Foundational Stage (Level 1)**: Concrete objects, 1-to-1 correspondence, subitizing, number bonds to 10, spatial shapes, repeating patterns.
2. **Preparatory Stage (Levels 2–3)**: Place value up to 3-digits, expanded notation, two- and three-digit operations with regrouping, multiplication as repeated addition and arrays, equal sharing division with remainders, fractions as equal parts of a whole, analog time, currency calculations.
3. **Middle Stage (Levels 4–6)**: Multi-digit fluency, divisibility rules, factors, primes and the Sieve of Eratosthenes, unlike fractions and decimals, percentages and proportional reasoning, directed integers and one-step algebraic equations, geometric angle theorems, Cartesian coordinates (Quadrants I–IV), data distributions.
4. **Secondary Foundations (Levels 7–8)**: Complex rational arithmetic, laws of exponents with negative powers, scientific notation, two-step linear equations, variables on both sides, algebraic identities ((a+b)², (a-b)², a²-b²), factorisation, Pythagorean theorem, circle and cylinder mensuration, direct and inverse proportion, simple and compound interest, arithmetic progressions, deductive logic.
